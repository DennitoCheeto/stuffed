How the plushies move

  • Plushies are built from separate pieces (feet, body, hood, flaps, beak). We move the pieces like a puppet; no frame-by-frame drawing.
  • Walking: the body waddles side to side and bobs, the feet step one after another, and the floppy parts (peel flaps, arms) swing a moment later than the body, which makes them feel soft.
  • Squash and stretch: plushies squish when they land or get hit and stretch when they dash. Stuffed animals are soft, so they can exaggerate this.
  • Zombies fall apart: because they're made of pieces, a dead zombie can burst into its pieces plus a cloud of stuffing.
