# 04 — Hero variety in bullet heavens (short research)

_2026-11-18, input for spec 08 (playable plushies). Short desk research; sources are guides, wikis and review
summaries, so treat them as signals, not facts._

## What makes heroes feel different

1. **A rule that bends, not a few percent.** Brotato's characters are loved because each one *breaks a normal
   rule*: Ghost trades armour for dodge, One Armed holds only one weapon but attacks much faster, Demon pays with
   health instead of materials. The same weapons then play very differently.
2. **Plain stat passives go unnoticed.** Vampire Survivors players say the base characters' passives "weren't
   really noticeable"; reviewers note its characters are mostly "small sprite animations and stat tweaks", and some
   become redundant once a better one with the same idea is unlocked.
3. **A signature move gives identity.** HoloCure gives every character a unique starting weapon, three own skills
   and a special attack (Fauna's healing tree, Mumei's screen clear, Reine's charged punch). Guides say the key to
   each character is "playing into its central gimmick".
4. **A shared pool makes heroes converge.** Halls of Torment classes share one ability pool; one review says they
   "can play similarly" and magic "doesn't vary a great deal". The hero's own rule must matter for the *whole* run,
   not just the first minute.
5. **Strength + weakness.** Brotato and Halls of Torment pair each strength with a clear cost (Warlock: more
   damage, less max HP). It keeps heroes sideways (vision §6.3) instead of "the better Banane".
6. **Unlocks should be a clear action, not a chore.** Halls of Torment's better classes take 2-3 hours of quests;
   Megabonk's achievement gates are a known complaint (research 01). Our "rescue = unlock" is a visible, in-run
   goal, which is the good kind.

## What this means for us

- Each plushie gets **its own starting weapon + one passive that changes a rule** (often the dash, like Banane's
  Slippery), plus **one clear strength and one clear weakness** in its stats.
- Keep the passive relevant all run (aura, dash, level-up trigger), so the shared card pool doesn't wash it out.
- Unlock = rescue and move in (already an in-run goal). No achievement lists.
- Balance stays in data; the playtest decides the numbers.
